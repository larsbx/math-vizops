Ray Address Atlas

Every number here is exact: ray addresses p/q and their types (l, k) under doubling, the catalogue counts the Möbius identity predicts, kneading sequences and internal addresses, the separator prefixes and the pairs they leave undecided, and dyadic boxes with the collisions they exclude. Three views show them: the parameter plane, the circle the addresses actually live on, and the finite incidence package that stands in for a point.

What is exact and what is not. Every exact number here is printed by pixi run atlas-dataset, the canonical implementation, and checked object by object against independent Python oracles. The positions are not: each one is a conventional numeric computation, a traced parameter ray finished by Newton on the equation the point satisfies, and they are computed by vizops, not by the research repository. Associating an address with a parameter is the imported landing theorem. finite-mandelbrot-research computes no parameter locations and claims none — no module under kernel/ may produce a floating-point number at all, which is why the positions are computed outside it. What that repository does locate is a box: an exclusion certificate is an exact dyadic rectangle, and what it proves is stated in its own row.

Inspector

The circle of addresses

The other plane. Every address is a point of Z/den on the circle of external angles, and doubling is the dynamics on it. A separator is a pair of rays declared to land together; it cuts the circle, and two addresses are separated when some separator puts them on opposite open sides. The extractor asks which pairs no iterate of doubling ever separates — those are the obstruction, and the cyclic ones are drawn heavy.

A point that is not a point

The third object, and the one the repository is really about. Nothing here may be an analytic singleton, so what a certificate emits is a PointVertex: a finite vertex whose carrier is exactly three other finite vertices — the root handle of the polynomial over a box, the set of ray addresses, and the dyadic box itself. Below it is the chain that has to pass before anything is emitted, and the two steps at the end that do not pass.
