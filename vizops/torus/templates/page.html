Rauzy fractals on the 2-torus

For a unimodular alphabet-3 Pisot substitution, projecting the stepped line along the frequency vector onto H = {Σxi = 0} gives the discrepancy vectors Dn = pn − n f. Mod Hℤ ≅ ℤ², they are the orbit of the toral rotation by −(f0, f1). Every number below is the evidence bundle's; the page draws it.

diagnostic · a lift profile certifies no covering degree

1 · in HThe Rauzy fractal, coloured by subtile

Each point is one discrepancy vector, drawn at (x, y)/2coord_bits in the coordinates (Dn0, Dn1), and coloured by the letter that follows it: the subtiles R(0), R(1), R(2).

The faint lines are the lattice ℤ². The picture is a linear image of the contracting-plane picture, so lengths and angles in it carry no meaning.

2 · on T²Its fold onto the torus, coloured by lift count

Each cell of the grid on T² = H/Hℤ is coloured by the number of distinct ℤ²-lifts the sample meets there, read from lifts[cx·G + cy]. The dots are the same points folded mod ℤ².

cell (cx, cy) = (⌊G·frac D₀⌋, ⌊G·frac D₁⌋) assigned only when the whole enclosure lies in it

Covering degree 1 is a theorem for Tribonacci, and its profile still shows boundary cells with two or three lifts. A finite sample at one resolution neither certifies nor refutes covering degree 1.

3 · embeddedThe same fold on a torus surface

The cell (u, v) ∈ [0, 1)² sits at ((R + r cos 2πv) cos 2πu, (R + r cos 2πv) sin 2πu, r sin 2πv). The embedding is a display aid; the colours are the lift profile of view 2. Drag to turn it.

4 · the corpusRotation vectors of every unimodular specimen

Each dot is one specimen of the standing corpus at ρσ = −(F0, F1)/2scale_bits mod ℤ², from the certified floors Fi = ⌊2scale_bits fi⌋. ρ depends only on the incidence matrix, so specimens sharing one share a point: each point is drawn as a small cluster, one dot per specimen, centred on its ρ. Since f0 + f1 = 1 − f2 < 1, every ρ = (1 − f0, 1 − f1) lies above the diagonal u + v = 1; the shaded half is unreachable.

A low one-lift share is resolution-limited: adding points only finds more lifts, and refining the grid is bounded by point density. It is not evidence of covering degree ≥ 2.
