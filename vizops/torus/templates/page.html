Rauzy fractals, on the torus they tile

For a unimodular Pisot substitution σ on three letters, the stepped line of a fixed point stays near the expanding line ℝv. Projecting it along that line onto the plane H = {x₀+x₁+x₂ = 0} gives the discrepancy vectors Dₙ = pₙ − n·f, where f is the letter-frequency vector, and their closure is the Rauzy fractal. Modulo the lattice H ∩ ℤ³ ≅ ℤ², the same points form the orbit of a rotation of the 2-torus by −(f₀, f₁). Every point drawn here was computed exactly in PSC's Mojo kernel and is shown as it was emitted.

Each point is (Dₙ₀, Dₙ₁) for one prefix of the fixed point. It is coloured by the next letter xₙ, so the three colours are the subtiles R(0), R(1) and R(2).

x ↦ x − |x|₁ f (projection along v onto H) Dₙ = pₙ − n f ∈ R(xₙ)

Switch on the translates to see how copies of R by ℤ² fit together. The Pisot conjecture for σ says these copies tile the plane: they cover it, overlapping only on boundaries.

Plate II The fold onto T² = H / ℤ², coloured by lift count

Divide the torus into a grid. For each cell, the kernel counted how many distinct integer translates of that cell the sampled fractal meets. A cell inside a tile has one lift, and a cell on a tile boundary can have two or three.

If R covered the torus twice almost everywhere (covering degree 2), most cells would show two lifts. The kernel's negative control is a synthetic double cover, and it does show two everywhere.

A finite sample at one resolution certifies no covering degree. The kernel leaves out any point whose exact enclosure straddles a grid line, and counts it as undecided.

Plate III The orbit on the torus surface

drag to turn

The same folded points, wrapped onto a torus in space by (u, w) ↦ ((2 + cos 2πw) cos 2πu, (2 + cos 2πw) sin 2πu, sin 2πw). Press play to add the points in order of n. Each step advances the rotation by −(f₀, f₁), the straight-line flow along v in T³ cut by this transversal, and the orbit fills the surface evenly.

Plate IV The corpus: one rotation vector per substitution

low one-lift sharehigh one-lift shareaxes: f₀, f₁

Each unimodular specimen of the standing 4,554-substitution corpus is placed at its frequency pair (f₀, f₁). Its rotation is minus that pair, a point of T². The colour is the share of occupied cells with one lift in that specimen's own sample. Hover over a point to see the substitution.

Non-unimodular specimens are refused upstream. Their quotient carries a p-adic factor, a solenoid rather than a torus, so they are counted but not placed.
