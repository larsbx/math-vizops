Rauzy tile contacts, decided exactly

For a unimodular Pisot substitution σ on three letters, an overlap (i, j, t) of PSC's box automaton has t = ⟨ℓ, w⟩ for one integral w ∈ ℤ³. In the plane H = {x₀+x₁+x₂ = 0} it is a contact: the subtile R(i) translated by P(w) = w − |w|₁ f meets R(j). The contacts are the descendants of the box's cycles, and those with |w|₁ = 0 are the neighbours of R in its ℤ² tiling. The sampled lift profile on the torus page gives way here to these exact sets.
